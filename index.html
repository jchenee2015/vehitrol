What we do

Engineering services

Specialized consulting across the full lifecycle of electrified vehicle development — from concept to calibration.

Automotive Control Systems

Model-based design, control architecture, and calibration for powertrain, chassis, and ADAS applications.

Battery Management Systems

BMS design and validation: cell monitoring, state estimation (SOC/SOH/SOE), balancing strategies, and safety logic.

State Estimation

Kalman filters, observers, and data-driven estimators for real-time state and parameter estimation in dynamic systems.

Vehicle Electrification

System-level consulting for HEV, PHEV, and BEV architectures — energy management, component sizing, and integration.

Optimization & Learning

Applied machine learning and optimization for energy efficiency, adaptive control, and predictive diagnostics.

Robotics

Motion planning, kinematics, and control design for autonomous and semi-autonomous robotic systems across industrial and mobility applications.

SLAM

Simultaneous localization and mapping for ground vehicles and mobile robots — sensor fusion, loop closure, and real-time map building using lidar, camera, and IMU data.

Renewable Integration

Control and optimization strategies for integrating solar, wind, and energy storage into vehicle and grid-connected systems — including power flow management, grid interaction, and hybrid energy architectures.

How we work

Rigorous by design

Good control systems engineering is invisible — the vehicle does exactly what the driver intends, the battery lasts as long as it should, the software ships without surprises.

Vehitrol brings academic rigor and production discipline to every engagement, whether that means a single algorithm review or end-to-end system development.

  • i.
    Define the problem precisely Identify requirements, constraints, and failure modes before any design work begins.
  • ii.
    Model before you build Physics-based and data-driven models inform architecture and expose edge cases early.
  • iii.
    Validate at every level Unit tests, HIL simulation, and calibration close the loop between design intent and hardware behavior.
  • iv.
    Deliver production-ready work Documentation, code quality, and handoff support that your team can build on.

About

The engineer behind the work

Dr. Jun Chen received his B.S. in Automation from Zhejiang University in 2009, and his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science from Iowa State University in 2014. He went on to work as a research scientist at Idaho National Laboratory (2014–2016) and as a senior control engineer at General Motors (2017–2020).

Since 2020, Dr. Chen has been an Associate Professor in the ECE Department at Oakland University, where his research spans advanced control and optimization, model predictive control, artificial intelligence, and stochastic hybrid systems — with applications in automotive and energy systems.

Through Vehitrol LLC, he brings that combination of industrial and academic experience directly to clients: rigorous methods, production awareness, and domain depth that is hard to find in a single engagement.

Dr. Chen is a Senior Member of the IEEE and serves or has served as associate editor for IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Letters, ASME Journal of Dynamic Systems, Measurement, and Control, and several flagship IEEE conferences.
